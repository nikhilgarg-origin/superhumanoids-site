Super·Humanoids

Robots that generalize, not robots that repeat.

Artila Robotics is building foundation models for general-purpose humanoid robots — one model family for perception, locomotion and manipulation, learned rather than programmed task by task.

The problem

Every robot that works today works because someone told it exactly what to do.

Change the object, the lighting, the surface, the height of the table — and it stops. Decades of robotics have produced machines that are extraordinary at one task and helpless at the next. General-purpose robots need models that carry capability across tasks and across bodies, the way language models carry it across domains. That is the problem we work on.

Approach

01

One policy, many tasks

A single model family spanning perception, whole-body control and manipulation — trained across tasks rather than engineered for each one.

02

Simulation at scale

Policies learn across massively parallel physics simulation, where a robot can attempt a task more times in an hour than a human could in a lifetime.

03

Closing the reality gap

A policy is only as good as its transfer to the physical world. Our development loop is built around narrowing that gap, not around benchmark scores.
