General-purpose robotics

Robots that generalize, not robots that repeat.

Artila Robotics builds foundation models for general-purpose humanoid robots — systems that learn manipulation and locomotion from large-scale simulation and real-world interaction, then carry that capability across tasks and across hardware.

What we build

Foundation models

One model family spanning perception, whole-body control and manipulation — trained across tasks rather than programmed per task.

Simulation at scale

Policies train across massively parallel physics simulation on GPU clusters. Sim-to-real transfer is the core engineering problem, not an afterthought.

Hardware in the loop

Models are validated on physical platforms. Teleoperation and on-robot telemetry feed continuously back into training.
